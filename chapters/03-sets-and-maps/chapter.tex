\chapter{Objects, maps, and information}\label{ch:maps}
\goal{Use sets to say exactly which objects a question is about, and functions to describe how inputs are turned into outputs. Prove facts about sets and functions by unpacking their definitions, one membership at a time. Decide whether a function loses information by sending different inputs to the same output, and notice when a proof needs a property only at the points it actually uses.}
