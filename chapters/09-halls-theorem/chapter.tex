\chapter{Hall's theorem: a complete proof apprenticeship}\label{ch:hall}
\goal{Prove Hall's theorem, which says exactly when every student can be given a different acceptable task. Read a proof with several stages and rebuild it yourself. Prove the theorem twice, once with the search procedure of Chapter~\ref{ch:matching} and once by strong induction, and see that both proofs turn on the same obstruction: a group of students who between them accept too few tasks. Learn how the case of equality in an inequality can show where to split a problem into smaller ones.}
