\chapter{How a proof finds its next step}\label{ch:proof}
\goal{Find the next step of a proof by working backward from what you must show and forward from what you know. Use direct proof, proof of the contrapositive, proof by contradiction, and separate proofs of existence and uniqueness, and learn when each helps. Prove that every integer is even or odd, and that $\sqrt2$ is irrational. Read a long proof by checking that each earlier result it uses really applies.}
