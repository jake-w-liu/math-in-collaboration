\chapter{A complete AI-assisted investigation}\label{ch:capstone}
\goal{Take the contraction theorem of Chapter~\ref{ch:contraction} and change one of its assumptions: suppose that every update is carried out with a small error. Test a first guess about what happens, find a statement that is actually true, prove it, ask whether its constant can be improved, and check it against an exact computation. Then write the investigation up so that another reader can check every claim in it, and plan an investigation of your own.}
