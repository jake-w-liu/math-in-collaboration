\chapter{The contraction theorem: an unfamiliar proof made readable}\label{ch:contraction}
\goal{Prove the contraction theorem: a map of a nonempty complete metric space to itself that multiplies every distance by at most a fixed factor below one has exactly one fixed point, and repeating the map from any starting point approaches it. Prove existence, uniqueness, convergence and the error bounds as four separate claims with the tools of Chapter~\ref{ch:limits}. Use the error bounds to guarantee the accuracy of an approximation without knowing the exact answer, and remove the hypotheses one at a time to see why each is needed.}
