\chapter{Induction, invariants, and progress}\label{ch:induction}
\goal{Prove a statement for every natural number by checking a first case and one general step. Use an invariant to show what a process can never change, and a nonnegative integer that keeps decreasing to show that a process must stop. See how choosing a more general statement to prove can make an induction proof simpler.}
