\chapter{Induction, invariants, and progress}\label{ch:induction}
\goal{Prove a statement about every natural number from a first case and one general step, and use strong induction, whose step may use all the earlier cases. Use an invariant to show what a process can never change, and a decreasing nonnegative integer to show that a process must stop; Euclid's algorithm for the greatest common divisor uses both. See how a more general statement can make an induction proof simpler.}
