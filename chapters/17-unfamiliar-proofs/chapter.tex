\chapter{Entering an unfamiliar proof without starting over}\label{ch:bridge}
\goal{Draw a prerequisite map for one particular proof, learn only the pieces of it that are new to you, and then rebuild the whole proof. The proof in this chapter shows that every sequence in a complete, totally bounded metric space has a convergent subsequence. Then use the same method to begin reading a formalized research proof, without mistaking its final file for all of its mathematics.}
