\chapter{Averaging, randomness, and existence}\label{ch:probability}
\goal{Treat finite probability as weighted counting: list the outcomes, give each one a weight, and add. Use averages and the union bound to prove that an object exists, without mistaking an average for a guarantee about every outcome. Turn one such existence proof into a step-by-step construction, and bound how often a nonnegative quantity can be much larger than its average.}
