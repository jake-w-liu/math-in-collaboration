\chapter{Assignments, repairs, and obstructions}\label{ch:matching}
\goal{Describe an assignment problem precisely as a graph. See why choices that look reasonable one at a time can lead to a dead end, and learn how to repair an earlier choice without creating a new conflict. Build a search procedure that either enlarges the current assignment or, when it fails, finds a group of students who together accept too few tasks, which proves that no complete assignment exists.}
