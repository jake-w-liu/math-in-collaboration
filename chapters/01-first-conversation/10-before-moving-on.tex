\section*{Before moving on}
Close the book and explain, in your own words, why the sum of two odd integers is even. You do not need the exact wording of the proof, but you should be able to say what the witnesses $k$ and $\ell$ are, why they need different letters, and why the last step is a return to the definition of even. You should also be able to say how a definition, a checked example, and a claim about every allowed input differ from one another. These distinctions stay just as important later in the book, when the objects become much less familiar than odd and even numbers.
