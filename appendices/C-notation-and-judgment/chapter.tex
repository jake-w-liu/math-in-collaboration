\chapter{A compact guide to notation and mathematical judgment}\label{app:guide}
This appendix gathers the notation the book uses (Section~\ref{appC:sec:notation}), the judgment that guides how its proofs are read and found (Sections~\ref{appC:sec:structures}--\ref{appC:sec:record}), a language map for research papers (Section~\ref{appC:sec:language}), and a compact protocol for working with an AI assistant (Section~\ref{appC:sec:ai}). It also includes a suggested rubric for the capstone investigation and ends with a description of what the book aims to leave you able to do.
