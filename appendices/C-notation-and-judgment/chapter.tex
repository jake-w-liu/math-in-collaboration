\chapter{A compact guide to notation and mathematical judgment}\label{app:guide}
This appendix gathers two things that the book develops gradually: its notation (Section~\ref{appC:sec:notation}), and the judgment that guides how its proofs are read and found (Sections~\ref{appC:sec:structures}--\ref{appC:sec:record}). It ends with a suggested rubric for the capstone investigation and a description of what the book aims to leave you able to do.
