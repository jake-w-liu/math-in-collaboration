\chapter{Solutions for Chapters 13--18}\label{app:solc}
This appendix contains the solutions for Chapters~\ref{ch:probability}--\ref{ch:capstone}: finite probability, sign sequences, the two chapters on Lean, reading an unfamiliar proof, and the capstone investigation. Several solutions for Weeks~15 and~16 are written in Lean; Section~\ref{appD:sec:lean} explains how to have Lean check them on your own computer. The independent capstone of Section~\ref{ch18:sec:independent} is your own project, so it has no model solution here; Section~\ref{appE:sec:rubric} suggests how it can be assessed.
