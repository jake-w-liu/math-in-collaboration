\chapter{Solutions for Chapters 13--18}\label{app:solc}
This appendix contains the solutions for Chapters~\ref{ch:probability}--\ref{ch:capstone}: finite probability, sign sequences, the two chapters on Lean, reading an unfamiliar proof, and the capstone investigation. As in Appendix~\ref{app:sola}, the section headed ``Week~$N$'' holds the solutions for Chapter~$N$. Several solutions for Weeks~15 and~16 are written in Lean; Appendix~\ref{app:practical} explains how to run them on a computer.
