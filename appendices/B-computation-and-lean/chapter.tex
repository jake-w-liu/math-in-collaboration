\chapter{Running the examples on a computer}\label{app:practical}
None of the proofs in this book needs a computer, but a computer can help in two ways: it can carry out a finite experiment exactly, and it can check a formal proof. This appendix explains the small part of the programming language Python that the book's experiments use, including exact arithmetic with fractions (Sections~\ref{appB:sec:python} and~\ref{appB:sec:fractions}); gives three complete programs, for Chapters~\ref{ch:signs}, \ref{ch:matching} and~\ref{ch:capstone} (Section~\ref{appB:sec:experiments}); and shows how to check the Lean examples of Chapters~\ref{ch:lean1} and~\ref{ch:lean2} (Section~\ref{appB:sec:lean}).
