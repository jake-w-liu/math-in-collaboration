\chapter{Solutions}\label{app:solutions}
This appendix contains complete solutions to every exercise in the book, grouped by chapter: the section headed ``Chapter~$N$'' holds the six solutions for Chapter~$N$.

Make a serious attempt before you read a solution, and then read it to learn from it, not only to confirm an answer. If your attempt succeeded, compare it with the solution anyway: did you prove exactly what was asked, in every case, including boundary cases such as zero or the empty set? If it stalled, find where your argument stopped and the idea that carries the solution past that point; then close the book and write the whole argument in your own words. Either way, finish by trying a small variation, such as different numbers or one hypothesis removed, and see whether the idea still works.

Where an exercise asks you to invent an example or explain something in your own words, the solution gives one model answer; a different answer is just as correct if it meets every requirement of the exercise.

Several solutions for Chapters 15 and 16 are written in Lean; Section~\ref{appB:sec:lean} explains how to have Lean check them on your own computer. The independent capstone of Section~\ref{ch18:sec:independent} is your own project, so it has no model solution; Section~\ref{appC:sec:rubric} suggests how to assess it.
