\chapter{A compact guide to notation and mathematical judgment}\label{app:guide}
This appendix gathers in one place two things that the book develops gradually: its notation, and the judgment needed to use that notation well. Section~\ref{appE:sec:notation} is a table of the main symbols, each with the place where it is explained. Sections~\ref{appE:sec:structures}--\ref{appE:sec:record} collect the ways of reading and reasoning that run through the chapters: what the main structures of the book allow, how to choose the next move in a proof, how to turn a reading difficulty into a precise question, and how to keep a record of a proof you are studying. Section~\ref{appE:sec:rubric} suggests a rubric for the capstone investigation, and Section~\ref{appE:sec:finishing} describes what the course aims to leave you able to do.
