\section{What finishing the course should feel like}\label{appE:sec:finishing}
You do not need to remember every displayed formula in this book. What you should be able to do is this. Faced with a statement, you can say exactly what it requires. Faced with a proof you do not yet follow, you can find the place where your understanding stops, and ask for the one concept or step that would carry you past it. When an explanation arrives, from a book, a classmate or an assistant, you can test it on an example, see what role it plays in the whole proof, and say clearly what has not yet been established.

The next unfamiliar theorem you meet will still contain mathematics you have not seen. The difference is that you now have a way in: identify the structures involved, lay out which steps depend on which, learn the pieces you are missing, and return to the argument. Working with an AI assistant is worthwhile when what it produces helps you understand and gives you something you can check. It is not worthwhile when it merely fills an empty page with text that looks like mathematics.
