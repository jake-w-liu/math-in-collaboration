\chapter{Running the examples on a computer}\label{app:practical}
None of the proofs in this book requires a computer. Computers are useful in two other ways: they can carry out a finite experiment exactly, and they can check a formal proof. This appendix gives the small amount of Python needed to repeat the experiments in the book and explains how to start a Lean file for the examples of Chapters~15 and~16. Every program shown here is complete; nothing else needs to be downloaded.
