\chapter{Solutions for Chapters 1--6}\label{app:sola}
This appendix and the next two contain complete solutions to every exercise in the book. The solutions are grouped by week, and the section headed ``Week~$N$'' holds the six solutions for Chapter~$N$. Here you will find the solutions for Chapters~\ref{ch:start}--\ref{ch:structure}: first proofs about even and odd numbers, statements and logic, sets and maps, proof strategies, induction and invariants, and equivalence and symmetry.

Make a serious attempt at an exercise before you read its solution, and then read the solution to learn from it, not only to confirm a final answer. If your attempt succeeded, compare it with the solution anyway, and check that you proved exactly what the exercise asked, in every case it covers, including boundary cases such as zero or the empty set. If your attempt stalled, find the point in the solution where your own argument stopped, and identify the fact or idea that carries the solution past that point. Then close the book and write the whole argument again in your own words. In either case, finish by testing yourself on a small variation, such as different numbers or one hypothesis removed, and check whether the same idea still works.

Some exercises, here and in the next two appendices, ask you to invent an example or to explain something in your own words. For these, the solution gives one model answer. A different answer can be just as correct, provided it meets every requirement of the exercise.
