\chapter{Solutions for Chapters 1--6}\label{app:sola}
This appendix and the next two contain complete solutions to every exercise in the book. The solutions are grouped by week, and the section headed ``Week~$N$'' holds the solutions for Chapter~$N$. For instance, the section headed ``Week~3'' answers the six exercises of Chapter~\ref{ch:maps}, and the solution to Exercise~\ref{ex:3.2} is headed ``Solution to Exercise~3.2.'' This appendix covers Chapters~\ref{ch:start}--\ref{ch:structure}: first proofs about even and odd numbers, logic, sets and functions, proof strategies, induction, and equivalence and symmetry.

The solutions are written to teach, not only to confirm a final answer. Make a serious attempt at an exercise before you read its solution. If your attempt stalled, find the point in the solution where your own argument stopped, and identify the fact or idea that carries the solution past that point. Then close the book and write the whole argument again in your own words. Finally, test yourself on a small variation, such as different numbers or one hypothesis removed, and check whether the same idea still works.

Some exercises, here and in the next two appendices, ask you to invent an example or to explain something in your own words. For these, the solution gives one model answer. A different answer can be just as correct, provided it meets every requirement of the exercise.
